# Business Expense Policy

## Meals

Employees may claim up to $30 for lunch and $60 for dinner while traveling for
business.

Alcohol is not reimbursable unless the expense is part of an approved client
event.

## Hotels

The normal hotel reimbursement limit is $180 per night.

For travel to New York City, San Francisco, London, or Tokyo, the limit is
$260 per night.

## Receipts

Receipts are required for expenses of $25 or more.

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date.
