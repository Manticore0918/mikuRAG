# Employee Handbook

## Working Hours

Normal working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday through Friday.

Employees may begin work between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM, provided they complete
eight working hours and attend required meetings.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week.

New employees must complete their first 30 calendar days in the office before
using the remote-work policy.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Unused annual leave can be carried forward, but no more than five days may be
carried into the next year.
