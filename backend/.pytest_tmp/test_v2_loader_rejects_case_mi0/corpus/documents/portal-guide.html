Account setup checklist

  • Verify the email address.
  • Register a passkey.

Invoice export limits

Invoice exports support CSV and JSON formats.

A single export may contain at most 25,000 rows.

Alert routing list

  • Priority one alerts use SMS and a phone call.
  • Priority two alerts use email and team chat.
  • Priority three alerts use email only.

Search syntax

Use quotation marks for an exact phrase and prefix a label with tag: to filter by tag.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Press g then h to return home.
  • Press a question mark to open shortcut help.

Status badge meanings

Green means operational, amber means degraded, and red means unavailable.
