# People policy

## Annual leave carry-over

Employees may carry over at most five days of unused annual leave. Any balance above five days expires on 31 December unless Human Resources records a statutory exception.

## Remote equipment

Remote-work equipment costing more than SGD 1,800 requires approval from both the department head and Information Technology before purchase. Reimbursement approval after purchase is not valid.

## New starter sequence

Human Resources must confirm identity and payroll details before a new starter's first day. Security must enroll the starter in multi-factor authentication within four hours of account activation.

## Learning allowance

Each employee has an annual learning allowance of SGD 1,200. Requests must be submitted by 30 November, and unused allowance does not roll into the next year.

## Contractor access

A contractor access grant requires a named employee sponsor and Security approval. The grant expires after eight hours and cannot be renewed without a new review.

## Expense submission

Employees must submit ordinary expense claims within twenty calendar days. Claims must include an itemized receipt and a business-purpose note.
