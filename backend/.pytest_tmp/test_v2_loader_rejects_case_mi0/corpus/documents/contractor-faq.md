# Contractor travel FAQ

## Remote equipment

Contractors may purchase remote-work equipment up to SGD 1,500 without prior approval.

## Lodging cap

Contractors are reimbursed up to SGD 180 per night for lodging. Amounts above SGD 180 require the employee's approval.

## Trip approval

A contractor trip can be approved by the contractor themself when the employee sponsor is unavailable.

## Expense deadline

Contractors must submit travel expenses within thirty calendar days after returning.
